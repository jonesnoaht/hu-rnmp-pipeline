## 1. Clarendon Fund

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Clarendon Fund Scholarships |
| **Eligibility** | All nationalities; apply by December or January deadline for any full-time or part-time Master's or DPhil at Oxford. No field-of-study restrictions. Automatically considered — no separate application. |
| **Amount** | Full course fees (university + college) + annual living costs grant of at least £15,009/year (2025–26 rate for full-time). Part-time DPhil: ≥£2,502/year + full fees. |
| **Duration** | Full period of fee liability — typically 3–4 years for DPhil. |
| **Deadline** | Course application deadline: **8 January 2027** (for Oct 2027 entry; some courses have December deadline — check course page). |
| **Funds data harmonization/bioinformatics?** | ✅ **Yes.** Open to all subjects. A DPhil in Biology, Genomic Medicine & Statistics, or Interdisciplinary Life Sciences at Oxford would be fully eligible. The research topic (data harmonization, sequencing data analysis, pathway modeling) is entirely compatible. |
| **URL** | https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon |

**Notes:** ~140 new scholarships/year. Awarded on academic excellence and potential. This is the primary Oxford-internal full-funding route — applying to the DPhil programme by the January deadline automatically enters you.

## 2. Rhodes Scholarship

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Rhodes Scholarship |
| **Eligibility** | Open to citizens of ~25+ constituencies worldwide (including USA). US applicants: must be aged 18–23 (or up to 26 if graduated later), hold or expect to hold a bachelor's before October 2027. Merit-based: literary & scholastic attainments, energy to use talents, truth, courage, devotion to duty, moral force of character. Must apply through country constituency. |
| **Amount** | Full Oxford course fees + annual stipend of **£20,400** (2025–26 rate) + visa/IHS fees + settling-in allowance + two economy flights (beginning/end of studies). Does not cover partners/dependents. |
| **Duration** | Up to **3 years** for DPhil (fees + stipend). Most full-time postgraduate courses eligible. |
| **Deadline** | **USA constituency: 7 October 2026** (11:59 PM ET). Global constituencies vary (typically Jul–Sep 2026). District interviews mid-November 2026. |
| **Funds data harmonization/bioinformatics?** | ✅ **Yes.** Open to nearly all full-time Oxford postgraduate courses. A DPhil in Biology (bioinformatics/computational biology), Genomic Medicine & Statistics, or Interdisciplinary Life Sciences would be eligible. The scholarship does not restrict research topic. |
| **URL** | https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/the-rhodes-scholarship/ |

**Notes:** This is the most prestigious fully-funded route to Oxford. US competition is fierce (~32 scholars/year from the US). Requires university endorsement. Noah's Track B Oxford connections could be an asset for the application narrative. Applications for October 2027 entry are now open.

## 3. Marshall Scholarship

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Marshall Scholarship |
| **Eligibility** | US citizens only. Must hold a first undergraduate degree from an accredited 4-year US institution before taking up the scholarship (Sept 2027). Must have graduated after April 2024. Minimum GPA **3.7** (4.0 scale). Cannot already hold a British degree. Must apply through one of 8 US regions. |
| **Amount** | Full university fees + living expenses (~£1,452/month, ~£17,400/year; ~£1,781/month in London) + annual book grant + thesis grant + research and daily travel grants + fares to/from the US + dependent spouse contribution where applicable. |
| **Duration** | **One-Year** (12 months, non-extendable) or **Two-Year** (22 months, extendable to a third year for PhD on limited basis). Third-year extensions sometimes available (including Ray Dolby-Marshall). |
| **Deadline** | **29 September 2026** (5pm, time zone of endorsing institution); institution endorsement due 1 October 2026. Applications open 29 May 2026. |
| **Funds data harmonization/bioinformatics?** | ✅ **Yes.** Unrestricted field of study. Can be used at any UK university including Oxford. For a full DPhil (3–4 years), the 2-year Marshall would need leverage into Oxford-internal funding (Clarendon, departmental studentship) for years 3–4 — many Marshall Scholars pair it with Clarendon or Wellcome funding. |
| **URL** | https://www.marshallscholarship.org/ |

**Notes:** Up to 50 scholars/year. Requires institutional endorsement (UF can endorse). Strong fit for Noah as a US citizen pursuing UK graduate study. Best used for the first 2 years of DPhil, combined with Oxford-internal funding for remaining years.
