| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Name** | AGSAA Rare Researcher Early Career Researcher (ECR) Grant |

| **Deadline** | Rolling / irregular cycles (last confirmed RFA cycle: 2022; monitor website for re-openings) |
| **DNA Repair / rNMP Relevance** | **Direct.** RNASEH2 mutations (RNASEH2A, RNASEH2B, RNASEH2C) are the most common cause of AGS. Proposals linking rNMP incorporation, RNASEH2 function, or hydroxyurea synergy to AGS pathogenesis would be directly on-mission. |
| **URL** | https://agsaa.org/ (grants page: https://agsaa.org/news/2022/6/23/researchers-not-so-rare) |
| **Status** | Small foundation; limited funding but exceptionally high topical match. Contact directly for upcoming cycles. |
