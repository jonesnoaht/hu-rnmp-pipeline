## Detailed Fellowship Profiles

---

### 1. NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Name** | NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP) |
| **Eligibility** | U.S. citizens, nationals, or permanent residents. Must be in first year of a research-based master's or doctoral program (or undergraduate senior). Must not have completed more than one academic year of graduate study. Only one application allowed per competition. |
| **Amount** | $37,000 annual stipend + $16,000 cost-of-education allowance (tuition/fees) per year — total ~$53,000/yr; ~$159,000 over 3 years |
| **Duration** | 3 years of support, usable over a 5-year fellowship period |
| **Deadline** | **November 10–14, 2025** (varies by field — Life Sciences: Nov 10; CISE/Materials/Psychology/SBE: Nov 12; Engineering: Nov 13; Chemistry/Geo/Math/Physics: Nov 14). Reference letters due Nov 7, 2025. **Annual cycle.** |
| **Focus Area** | All STEM fields supported by NSF, including: Computer and Information Science & Engineering (CISE), Mathematical Biology, Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, Biological Sciences. Applicable to data harmonization, multi-omics, and computational biology research. |
| **URL** | https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/grfp-nsf-graduate-research-fellowship-program |
| **Relevance to Noah** | ⭐⭐⭐ **Top priority.** Funds graduate-level computational biology/bioinformatics work directly. Portable fellowship. Apply early in PhD. Life Sciences or CISE track both viable for data harmonization/multi-omics. |

### 2. HHMI Gilliam Fellows Program

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Name** | HHMI Gilliam Fellows Program |
| **Eligibility** | Second- or third-year PhD students in basic biological/biomedical sciences (including dual-degree MD-PhD, DVM-PhD) at an HHMI-eligible institution. Must have at least 2 full years of PhD study remaining. U.S. citizens, permanent residents, DACA recipients, and eligible international students. Must apply **with** their faculty PhD advisor as a pair. |
| **Amount** | $65,000 per year for up to 3 years (paid to the institution; covers stipend, discretionary allowance, and institutional allowance for tuition/fees). Eligible for additional 4 years of postdoc support. |
| **Duration** | Up to 3 years of PhD support (plus optional 4-year postdoc phase if pursuing academic research career) |
| **Deadline** | Application opens September 1, 2026; **application deadline: September 9, 2026** (annual cycle) |
| **Focus Area** | Basic biological and biomedical sciences across all HHMI-supported disciplines. Supports research in genetics, molecular biology, cell biology, genomics, computational approaches to biology. Requires commitment to advancing excellence and inclusion in science. |
| **URL** | https://www.hhmi.org/programs/gilliam-fellows |
| **Relevance to Noah** | ⭐⭐⭐ **High.** Funds during PhD. DNA repair/genomics/sequencing research fits HHMI's scope. Specific emphasis on mentorship and inclusive science. Requires advisor co-application. Computational biology research in an HHMI-eligible lab is eligible. |

### 3. Ford Foundation Fellowship Program (Predoctoral)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Name** | Ford Foundation Fellowship Program — Predoctoral (administered by National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine) |
| **Eligibility** | U.S. citizens/nationals/permanent residents. Must be committed to a career in teaching and research at the college/university level. Must be from a historically underrepresented group. Enrolled in or planning to enroll in a research-based PhD program. |
| **Amount** | ~$27,000 annual stipend for 3 years (historically; predoctoral). Plus institutional allowance and access to Ford Fellows conferences/mentoring. |
| **Duration** | 3 years |
| **Deadline** | Mid-December annually (historically ~December 17). **⚠️ PROGRAM SUNSETTING:** Ford Foundation announced wind-down by 2028. Final new cohorts were awarded in 2023–2024. All current fellows will be honored through end of terms. Check whether predoctoral cycle is still accepting applications for the current year. |
| **Focus Area** | Broad — all academic fields. Designed to increase diversity in higher education. Not specific to computational biology, but comp-bio PhD students from underrepresented backgrounds are eligible. |
| **URL** | https://sites.nationalacademies.org/pga/fordfellowships/ |
| **Relevance to Noah** | ⭐⭐ **Moderate.** If still accepting applications, this is a strong general graduate fellowship. Not comp-bio-specific. Must verify status due to wind-down (check National Academies site for current cycle availability). |

### 4. Schmidt Science Fellows

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Name** | Schmidt Science Fellows |
| **Eligibility** | Must be nominated by PhD-awarding institution (must be a nominating partner). Must be studying for a PhD in natural sciences (astronomy, biology, chemistry, physics, earth sciences), engineering, mathematics, or computing. Must complete all PhD requirements (including defense) between May 1, 2026 and June 30, 2027 (for 2027 cohort). Not for clinical-track MD-PhD or social science/economics students. |
| **Amount** | ~$100,000–$110,000 per year stipend (varies by year; recent reports cite $110,000/yr) |
| **Duration** | Up to 2 years of postdoctoral study |
| **Deadline** | Institutional nomination deadline: **May 15, 2026**. Full application deadline: **July 13, 2026**. Annual cycle. |
| **Focus Area** | Interdisciplinary science. Fellows must pivot to a **different discipline** from their PhD — e.g., a biology PhD could pivot to computational/data science, or a computing PhD could pivot to biology/multi-omics. Explicitly seeks fellows who "innovatively combine quantitative analyses and modeling to solve biological questions." |
| **URL** | https://schmidtsciencefellows.org/ |
| **Relevance to Noah** | ⭐⭐⭐ **High — but postdoc only.** Excellent fit if Noah's PhD is in biology/biochemistry and he wants to pivot deeper into computational biology/bioinformatics for postdoc. The interdisciplinary pivot requirement aligns perfectly with moving from wet-lab DNA repair work to computational multi-omics/data harmonization. Requires institutional nomination. |

### 5. Damon Runyon Quantitative Biology Fellowship Award

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Name** | Damon Runyon Quantitative Biology Fellowship Award |
| **Eligibility** | Open to computational scientists at the intersection of quantitative biology, data science, and cancer research. Must have completed a PhD, MD, MD/PhD, DDS, DVM, or DO. Degree must have been conferred no more than 5 years prior to application (Level 1). Must not have been in mentors' labs for more than 24 months prior to deadline. Requires **two mentors**: one in computational science ("dry lab") and one in cancer biology ("wet lab"). Non-U.S. citizens may apply if research is conducted at a U.S. institution. |
| **Amount** | Year 1: $76,000 stipend + $2,000 expenses. Year 2: $78,000 + $2,000. Year 3: $80,000 + $2,000. Total: ~$240,000 over 3 years. Plus $1,000/child dependent allowance and up to $100,000 medical school debt repayment (if eligible). No indirect costs/institutional overhead. |
| **Duration** | 3 years |
| **Deadline** | **December 1, 2026** at 4:00 PM Eastern Time (annual). Submitted via Proposal Central. |
| **Focus Area** | Computational biology — explicitly for scientists trained in mathematics, computer science, physics, engineering, or related fields who apply quantitative analyses and modeling to solve biological questions, particularly in cancer. Encourages novel computational methods. Joint dry-lab/wet-lab mentorship required. |
| **URL** | https://www.damonrunyon.org/for-scientists/application-guidelines/quantitative |
| **Relevance to Noah** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Best fit for postdoc.** This is the single most targeted fellowship for someone doing computational biology at the intersection of data science and cancer biology. Noah's hydroxyurea/rNMP/DNA repair research is directly cancer-relevant. The dual-mentor requirement (one computational, one wet-lab) perfectly suits a data-harmonization + wet-lab hybrid career. **Postdoc-level only** — apply near PhD completion. |

## Additional Fellowships to Consider (Bonus)

These were not in the original list but are highly relevant to Noah's profile:

### NSF GRFP — already covered above (best graduate-level option)

### NIH F31 Predoctoral Fellowship
- **Eligibility:** PhD students in biomedical/behavioral research; U.S. citizens/permanent residents
- **Amount:** Stipend + tuition + fees (varies by institution; ~$45,000–$50,000 total/yr)
- **Duration:** Up to 5 years (typically 2–3 years)
- **Deadline:** Standard NIH deadlines (Apr 8, Aug 8, Dec 8 — annual cycle via parent announcement)
- **Focus:** Biomedical research including bioinformatics/computational biology
- **URL:** https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/pa-files/PA-21-049.html
- **Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐ Directly funds PhD-level computational biology research

### NDSEG (National Defense Science and Engineering Graduate) Fellowship
- **Eligibility:** U.S. citizens/nationals; first/second-year grad students in STEM
- **Amount:** Full tuition + fees + $38,400/yr stipend
- **Duration:** 3 years
- **Deadline:** November (annual)
- **URL:** https://ndseg.sysplus.com/
- **Relevance:** ⭐⭐ Broad STEM, could cover computational biology
