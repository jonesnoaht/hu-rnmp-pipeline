## 1. Google PhD Fellowship

- **URL:** https://research.google/programs-and-events/phd-fellowship/
- **Amount:** ~$85K/year for 2 years (US). Breakdown: $70K stipend + $15K for tuition/fees + personal equipment. Amounts vary by region.
- **Eligibility:** Full-time PhD students who have completed graduate coursework by the start of the fellowship. Must be nominated by their university (not direct application). Open to students in the US, Canada, Europe, Africa, Australia, East Asia, India, Latin America, Middle East, New Zealand, Southeast Asia.
- **Research areas:** Algorithms & Optimization, Computer Architecture, HCI, Machine Learning & ML Foundations, Natural Language Processing, Quantum Computing, Systems/Networking/Cloud Computing, **Health Research** (subtopics: Population health, Complex medical data, Consumer health).
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ⚠ **Partially.** There is an explicit "Health Research" track covering population health, complex medical data, and consumer health. Computational biology that applies ML/AI to health data would fit this track. Pure wet-lab or non-AI bioinformatics would be a weaker fit. Noah's work on harmonizing public sequencing data + DNA repair could fit if framed around complex medical data / ML for genomics.
- **Deadline (2026 cycle):** Applications open Mar 5, 2026; close **Apr 30, 2026**. Decisions by Aug 31, 2026. (Note: departmental internal nomination deadline is typically ~2 weeks earlier, ~Apr 15.)
- **Notes:** Nomination is by official university representative; contact your department/grad school for internal process. Must remain enrolled full-time throughout the fellowship.

## 2. Microsoft Research Fellowship

- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/microsoft-research-fellowship/
- **Amount:** Varies by region. US: $47,000; Canada: $47,000; Europe: $27,000; Africa/India/LatAm/East Asia: $17,000. One-time unrestricted gift paid to the institution. Includes travel funding for in-person collaboration with Microsoft Research.
- **Eligibility:** PhD students, postdocs, and faculty at accredited universities in eligible regions. Students must be actively pursuing a PhD. Proposals submitted in response to specific "Research Challenges" posted by Microsoft Research.
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ⚠ **Depends on the research challenge.** Microsoft posts specific challenges each cycle; some are AI/ML-focused, others in health/SaaS/systems. If a health or computational biology challenge appears, it would be eligible. No dedicated bioinformatics track; depends on the year's challenge themes.
- **Deadline (2026 cycle):** Submission deadline was Dec 16, 2025 (now closed). Next cycle expected ~Dec 2026. Check the "How to Apply" page for the next call.
- **How to Apply URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/microsoft-research-fellowship/how-to-apply/

## 3. Apple Scholars in AI/ML PhD Fellowship

- **URL:** https://machinelearning.apple.com/updates/apple-scholars-aiml-2026
- **Amount:** Full tuition and fees for 2 academic years + up to $40,000/year living stipend + $5,000/year research-related expenses. Total value: ~$90K+/year.
- **Eligibility:** PhD students nominated by their university (invited institutions only). Nominee must be enrolled full-time at the nominating university at the start of Fall of the fellowship year and expect to be enrolled through the following academic year. Should be entering their last 2–3 years of study. G1s (first-year students) not eligible. Each institution may nominate only 1 student.
- **Research areas:** AI/ML — machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, and related fields. Focus on AI for accessibility, on-device ML, and applied ML.
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ⚠ **Only if framed through AI/ML.** No dedicated bioinformatics track, but if your research explicitly applies ML/AI to biological data (e.g., ML for genomics/sequencing data), it could be positioned as AIML research. The program is primarily for ML researchers; pure computational biology without strong ML framing would be a poor fit.
- **Deadline (2026 cycle):** Nominations due **Sep 8, 2026** (institutional internal deadline ~Jul 2026). Contact your university's research office.
- **Contact:** aiml_scholars@apple.com

## 4. Meta Research PhD Fellowship

- **URL:** https://research.facebook.com/fellowship/ (main) · https://metaresearchphdfellowship.smapply.io/ (application portal)
- **Amount:** $42,000/year annual stipend + tuition and fees for 2 years.
- **Eligibility:** Open to full-time PhD students enrolled at an accredited university in any country, in any year of PhD study. Evaluation based on research statement, research impact, and recommendation letters. Encourages applicants from underrepresented minority groups.
- **Research areas:** Applied Meta Science, AI System Software/Infrastructure, Audio/Acoustic Signal Processing, Computational Social Science, Computer Vision, Confidential Computing, Economics and Computation, HCI/AR, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Networking & Connectivity, Privacy & Data Use, Programming Languages, Security & Privacy, Structural Engineering & Materials Science, Systems for ML.
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ❌ **No dedicated bioinformatics or health data track.** The closest categories would be "Machine Learning" (if your ML methods are novel enough) or "Computational Social Science" (if health data has social science framing). Noah's DNA repair / sequencing pipeline work would be a very weak fit unless reframed around novel ML methodology.
- **Deadlines (typical annual cycle):** Applications open Aug 3; close **Sep 20**; reference letters due Oct 6; winners notified ~Jan 18. (The 2026 cycle dates may shift slightly; check the SMApply portal.)

## 5. Amazon AI PhD Fellowship

- **URL:** https://www.amazon.science/news/amazon-launches-68-million-ai-phd-fellowship-program
- **Amount:** Full stipend + 20% tuition + student health insurance for up to 2 academic years (fall/spring semesters). Plus AWS credits (the overall program includes $24M/year in AWS credits across all fellows). Amazon reports $34M/year total ($10M student funding + $24M AWS credits).
- **Eligibility:** Full-time PhD students at one of **9 invited universities** (including JHU, UW, and others). Students in 3rd year or higher at time of award. Any citizenship/nationality eligible. Research must be in AI/ML focus areas: agentic systems, large language models/generative AI, machine learning systems/infrastructure, automated reasoning, computer vision, NLP.
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ⚠ **AI/ML only, and institution-limited.** No bioinformatics-specific track. If your institution is one of the 9 participating universities and your research can be framed as ML for genomics/health, it may be eligible. Not open to all institutions.
- **Deadline:** September (annually). Exact date TBD for next cycle. The 2025 deadline was Sep 14.
- **Notes:** This is a newer program (launched Oct 2025). Limited to 9 partner universities — check if your institution participates.

## 6. NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship

- **URL:** https://research.nvidia.com/graduate-fellowships
- **Amount:** Up to $60,000 per student (paid to the university, which administers the award).
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must have completed at least 1 year of PhD-level studies at time of application.
  - Majors in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, System Architecture, Electrical Engineering, or a **related area**.
  - Must be engaged in active research as part of PhD thesis.
  - Must be enrolled full-time during the award year (2026–2027 academic year).
  - Must be available for an **in-person summer internship** preceding the fellowship year at an NVIDIA research office (US, Canada, Germany, Sweden, Finland, Switzerland, Israel, or Taiwan).
  - May not be immediate family of a current NVIDIA employee.
- **Research areas:** Accelerated computing and its applications — AI, robotics, autonomous vehicles, GPU computing, graphics, and related fields. Evaluated on relevance to NVIDIA's research domains and how research might influence future GPU design/use.
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ⚠ **Must connect to GPU computing.** NVIDIA funds research that advances accelerated computing. If your bioinformatics pipeline/work involves GPU-accelerated computation (e.g., GPU-based sequence alignment, accelerated variant calling, parallel bioinformatics), it could qualify. Pure computational biology without a GPU computing angle would not fit. The "related area" language offers some flexibility.
- **Deadline:** **Sep 15** (annually, 3:00 PM Pacific Time). Submissions managed via an online portal. Requires: 2-page research proposal, CV, 2–3 recommendation letters (1 must be from thesis advisor), confirmation of summer internship availability.

## 7. Data Science for Social Good (DSSG) Fellowship

- **URL:** https://dssgfellowship.org/ (main, University of Chicago/CMU) · Also see Stanford, UBC, Vanderbilt, JHU variants.
- **Amount:** Stipend varies by location; typically $6,000–$14,000 for a 10–14 week full-time summer program. Covers travel, housing, and living expenses.
- **Eligibility:**
  - Full-time summer program (typically 10–14 weeks, May–July/August).
  - 2026 program: open to **graduate students** currently at a US university (at the main DSSG program). Some university-specific variants (e.g., UBC) accept undergraduates.
  - Fellows work in teams on real-world data science projects with government, nonprofit, or social impact organizations.
- **Research areas:** Machine learning, data science, AI applied to social good — education, healthcare, criminal justice, environment, urban planning, government transparency, etc.
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ✅ **Yes, if framed as social good.** Health-related projects are common. If your computational biology / sequencing data harmonization work can be positioned as a social good project (e.g., improving public health data infrastructure, democratizing access to genomic data for underserved communities, public health surveillance), it would fit well. This is a summer program, not a full-year fellowship.
- **Deadline (2026):** Extended deadline **Mar 6, 2026**. Apply at: https://dssgfellowship.org/apply-to-be-a-2026-dssg-summer-fellow/
- **Notes:** JHU hosted a DSSG cohort in 2026 (GovEx partnership). Stanford, UBC, Vanderbilt also run their own DSSG programs with separate deadlines.

## 8. Cohen Postdoctoral Fellowship

- **URL:** No single central URL — this is **not a single national fellowship**. Multiple institutions have independently named "Cohen Fellowships."
- **Notable examples found:**
  - **Donald J. Cohen Fellowship** (Emory/Children's Healthcare of Atlanta) — developmental neuroscience. Contact: cohen.fellowship@emory.edu
  - **Cohen Fellowship in Developmental Social Neuroscience** (Stanford) — autism/neuroscience postdoctoral.
  - **Debra and Ira Cohen Fellowship** (UIUC Siebel School) — bioinformatics/computational biology.
  - **Cohen Lab Postdoctoral Fellow** (University of Colorado) — biochemistry/molecular biology (endothelial cells).
- **Amount:** Varies widely by institution. Typically a standard postdoctoral salary ($50K–$65K/year) + benefits.
- **Eligibility:** Varies by institution. Most are postdoctoral (not pre-doctoral/graduate student). Research focus depends on the specific Cohen fund (neuroscience, bioinformatics, biochemistry, etc.).
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ✅ **Yes, for the right one.** The UIUC Cohen Fellowship and some Cohen Lab positions are directly in computational biology/bioinformatics. However, this is a **postdoc** fellowship, not a graduate fellowship — relevant for Noah's future postdoc planning, not current PhD funding.
- **Deadline:** Varies by institution; no central application.
- **Action item:** Search individual university postdoc listings for "Cohen Fellowship" + your area.

## 10. ACM SIGHPC Fellowships & Awards

- **URL:** https://www.sighpc.org/
- **Amount:** $15,000/year fellowship (for SIGHPC Fellows); $2,000 + plaque + travel support (for Doctoral Dissertation Award).
- **Eligibility (SIGHPC Fellowship):** PhD students studying anywhere in the world, researching high-performance computing (HPC). Evaluated on academic merit.
- **Eligibility (Doctoral Dissertation Award):** Students who have completed a PhD dissertation with HPC as a central research theme, within the last year.
- **Bioinformatics/Health data eligible?** ⚠ **Yes, if HPC-focused.** If your computational biology research involves high-performance computing (parallel/distributed computing for bioinformatics, GPU-accelerated genomics, large-scale data processing on HPC clusters), it qualifies. HPC in computational biology is a recognized subfield.
- **Deadlines:** Fellowship nominations typically due ~May 1 annually. Dissertation award: check SIGHPC website for exact deadline (~early 2026 for the 2026 award).
- **URL (Dissertation Award):** https://www.sighpc.org/opportunities/dissertation-award
